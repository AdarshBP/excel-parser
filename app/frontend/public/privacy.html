Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

Excel Parser accesses your data only to provide the service you requested:

  • Google account email — used solely to identify your Drive connection within the app. Never shared with third parties.
  • Google Drive files (read-only) — accessed only when you explicitly select a file through the Google Picker. We download the file to process it; we do not browse, index, or store your Drive contents beyond the selected files.
  • Workbook contents — the Excel/CSV data you upload or link is parsed and loaded into your configured database. No copy is retained after processing, except a temporary cache that is cleared on restart.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or training.
  • We do not access any Drive files you have not explicitly selected.

Data storage

Your Google OAuth tokens are stored server-side, encrypted, and never returned by the API. You can revoke access at any time from the Settings page or from your Google Account permissions.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the application administrator.
